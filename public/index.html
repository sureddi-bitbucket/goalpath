The page reads official filings and official statistics, then writes a study plan. It will not name a stock to buy, and it will not name a price at which to sell.

How a suggestion is made

  1. Your date decides how much can sit in stocks. A purchase next year is mostly cash. Income that may last decades keeps a stock share.
  2. The Treasury yield curve and the latest CPI reading set the illustration. They are context, not a forecast.
  3. Mutual funds and ETFs are examples of a category. The prospectus filed with the SEC is the document that counts.
  4. Adding, reducing, and rebalancing follow the date and a 5 percentage-point band. A headline is only a reason to open a filing.
